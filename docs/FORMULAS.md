# FactoryPulse formula reference

**Provenance rule.** Pragati AutoFab is fictional. No prototype value is a real factory measurement. Use these four labels when describing a number:

| Label | Meaning in this demo |
| --- | --- |
| **MEASURED** | Reserved for future timestamped physical meter, PLC, or sensor readings. **None are connected here**, even where Factory Setup says “direct meter.” |
| **CALCULATED** | Arithmetic on simulated power, jobs, events, or scenario inputs: energy, cost, SEC, residuals, savings, payback, and verification. Say “calculated from simulated data.” |
| **INFERRED** | Consumption without a direct meter, interpreted machine state/cause, or an evidence confidence score. These need field confirmation. |
| **ILLUSTRATIVE BENCHMARK** | Assumed power bands, first 14 healthy days, 312 production days/year, tariffs, CAPEX, intervention percentages, sensor cost, and simulated after-intervention changes. Sources: `data/demo-assumptions.json` and `data/interventions.ts`; they are not site/OEM/BEE validated. |

Generated five-minute kW samples and fabricated jobs are **ILLUSTRATIVE BENCHMARK** demo inputs; quantities computed from them are **CALCULATED**, and attribution or state interpretation is **INFERRED**.

“LIVE” advances a **simulation** by five factory minutes every four real seconds. The Energy Map uses ₹9.20/kWh from simulation assumptions for today's cost; Opportunities, What-If, SenseROI, and Verification use the editable/default ₹9/kWh decision tariff. Do not compare their rupee figures without aligning tariffs.

## Energy and production

- Five-minute sample: `energy_kWh = power_kW × 5/60 hours`; daily energy is the sum of samples. Tree parent power is simulated as `Σ(child_kW × (1 + connection_loss_factor)) / (1 − assumed_unallocated_fraction)`. Current demand is the latest main-incomer kW; peak demand is its daily maximum.
- `cost_₹ = energy_kWh × tariff_₹/kWh`. Displayed values are rounded after calculation.
- At a parent: `known_child_kWh = Σ(immediate child kWh)`; `unallocated_kWh = max(0, parent_kWh − known_child_kWh)`; `unallocated_% = unallocated_kWh / parent_kWh × 100`. The assumed tree gap includes modeled distribution loss and unattributed load; it is not a measured loss audit.
- `share_of_plant_% = node_kWh / main_incomer_kWh × 100`; `idle_energy_ratio_% = IDLE sample kWh / node_kWh × 100`; `baseline_delta_% = (current-to-date kWh − time-scaled healthy daily average kWh) / time-scaled average × 100`.
- `SEC = energy_kWh / useful_output`, withheld when output is zero. Factory output is finished good units; daily laser output is good cutting jobs; welding/bending output is good components; coating output is coated components; utility output is productive factory hours. Job-level laser SEC is kWh per good job; its separate map KPI also shows kWh per good component. `kWh/job = job energy / good jobs`; `kWh/good component = process energy / good components`.

## Production-aware baseline and diagnosis

- Use healthy observations from the first 14 simulated days, matching equipment and product/product mix, then shift, batch size, and operating duration. At least five comparables are required. The baseline is median SEC after excluding values outside `median ± max(8% of median, 3.5 × median absolute deviation)`.
- `expected_kWh = baseline_SEC × current useful output`; `residual_kWh = actual_kWh − expected_kWh`; `deviation_% = residual_kWh / expected_kWh × 100`. A positive residual is a deviation, **not automatically waste**.
- Production confidence is a weighted score: `0.35 × metering quality + 0.25 × min(comparables/12,1) + 0.25 × context match + 0.15 × baseline stability`. Inferred/unmetered or extrapolated comparisons are capped. A watch band is `max(8%, 200 × relative baseline spread)`; escalation also requires sufficient deviation, repeated comparable days, confidence, and an operating signal. “Confirmed Opportunity” still requires site review and savings verification.
- Machine state combines power band, active job, optional PLC signal, shift, and operator event. OFF/FAULT/SETUP take precedence; productive power plus production evidence gives PRODUCTIVE; energized nonproductive intervals become IDLE or STANDBY. A time-aligned necessary event marks necessary idle. Material waits, long job gaps, or after-shift power are candidates, capped by idle time above the healthy-day median; the rest is unexplained.
- `idle_kWh = average_idle_kW × idle_hours`; `candidate_avoidable_kWh = average_idle_kW × counted_avoidable_hours`; `candidate_cost = candidate_avoidable_kWh × tariff`. Monthly and annual estimates multiply the recent daily average by 26 and 312 days. Operator cause totals allocate this same candidate energy across time-aligned samples; they do not create another saving pool. Entered demo events take priority over generated events and can change a generic gap into a material-staging diagnosis.

## Decision Engine and What-If

- An intervention's annual relevant basis is `recent daily candidate idle kWh`, `daily machine kWh`, or `positive SEC residual kWh`, as specified in `data/interventions.ts`, multiplied by 312 days. `saving_low/high_kWh = basis × illustrative reduction_low/high`; midpoint is their average. `annual_₹_saving = midpoint_kWh × selected tariff`; `simple_payback_months = CAPEX / annual_₹_saving × 12` (no payback for nonpositive saving); `five_year_net_₹ = 5 × annual_₹_saving − CAPEX`. Zero-CAPEX positive-saving actions have immediate payback.
- Hard filters remove actions exceeding CAPEX, preferred payback, production risk, or the configured production/quality restrictions. The default strict rules hold coating schedule changes and actions requiring quality validation. Eligible actions score: readiness `/30` + diagnostic confidence `/25` + annual value `/20` (capped at ₹40,000/year) + payback `/10` + CAPEX fit `/10` + production risk `/5`. `diagnostic_confidence = issue_confidence × action_readiness`. Overview sums only the highest-ranked eligible action **per machine**, so alternatives are not double-counted. Impact and guardrails are illustrative, not proof of preserved production.
- What-If holds annual good jobs/components constant. LC idle Option A subtracts `diagnosed avoidable idle kWh/day × reduction%`; resulting idle hours subtract only counted avoidable hours. Auto-standby replaces candidate-window energy with `remaining candidate hours × new standby kW`. Replacement energy/day is `required productive hours × new productive kW + existing idle hours × new idle kW + existing other-state kWh`; required hours are `current jobs/day ÷ proposed jobs/hour`. To preserve modeled output **while retaining idle energy**, required productive hours must fit within current productive hours. Maintenance saving enters financial payback, not energy saving.
- Compressor scenario replaces candidate unload energy with `remaining candidate unload hours × new unload kW`. Coating scenario uses `annual good components ÷ effective components/batch` batches, with `proposed kWh = fixed kWh/batch × batches + variable kWh/component × components`. Effective batch size is the smaller of requested components and reference capacity × requested utilisation. These are deterministic scenarios, not a dispatch or thermodynamic model.
- What-If recommends operating first when a positive operational scenario captures **more than 50%** of replacement energy saving for **less than 10%** of its CAPEX, or when replacement fails the output test. Scenario costs, payback, and five-year benefit use the same formulas above; maintenance saving is added only to replacement financial saving.

## SenseROI and verification

- SenseROI confidence is a **separate illustrative evidence score**: start at 34 points; add up to 28 for direct measurement, 12 for jobs, 8 for operator context, 10 for healthy comparables, and 9 for repeated occurrence; feeder-only measurement adds 10 instead of 28, missing context/short history and a shared feeder subtract points. Clamp to 5–95%. The UI lists every point. `uncertainty = 1 − current confidence`; `confidence_gain = expected direct-meter confidence − current confidence`.
- `potential annual opportunity_₹ = max(0, latest daily SEC residual kWh) × 312 × ₹9`; `information_value_₹ = potential annual opportunity × confidence_gain`; `SensorROI = information_value / ₹12,000 temporary-meter cost`. A seven-day measurement is justified when gain is at least 10 percentage points and information value covers cost. **Information value is not cash saving.**
- Verification generates a seven-day after period by lowering selected diagnosed LC-01 material/scheduling idle samples to **0.2 kW**; jobs, good output, and rejects are copied unchanged. `SEC_before/after = period kWh / good jobs`; `adjusted_baseline_kWh = SEC_before × after good jobs`; `verified_kWh = adjusted_baseline_kWh − after kWh`; `verified_% = verified_kWh / adjusted_baseline_kWh × 100`; `SEC_improvement_% = (SEC_before − SEC_after) / SEC_before × 100`; `verified_₹ = verified_kWh × ₹9`. Annual equivalents multiply seven-day saving by `312/7`. Status is VERIFIED only for positive saving, lower idle hours, no reduction in good jobs, and no increase in rejection rate. **This verifies internal simulation arithmetic, not a real implemented intervention.**
